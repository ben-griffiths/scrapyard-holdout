SCRAPYARD HOLDOUT
Day 1Daylight

Hold the yard

Your crew does the work. You give the orders.

  1. Click Chop in the bottom bar and drag over trees (C). Use Scavenge on scrap (V). Your crew gathers it.
  2. Open Camp, Crafting or Defense in the bar (4–8) and click the map to place. Crew fetch materials and build.
  3. Everyone eats a meal at dawn. Build Garden Plots and a Campfire.
  4. Zombies come at night. Put up Barricades before dark. More survivors unlock more.

Speed and pause sit just under the top bar (1 2 3 · Space) · Crew button for jobs and the guide (Tab)
